The CIA triad

Confidentiality means only the right people can read data. Integrity means data is not changed without permission. Availability means systems work when needed. Every security control protects at least one of these.

Threat, vulnerability, risk

A vulnerability is a weakness, such as unpatched software. A threat is something that could exploit it. Risk is how likely and how damaging that would be.

Defence in depth

No single control is perfect, so layers are used: strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, updates, firewalls, backups, and monitoring.

Key terms

  • MFA: Using two or more proofs of identity.
  • Patch: An update that fixes a vulnerability.
  • Least privilege: Giving only the access needed.

Try it yourself

  2. Check that automatic updates are on.
  3. Name one threat, one vulnerability, and one risk in your daily life.
